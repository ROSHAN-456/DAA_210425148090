Mission Brief

MISSION MIN-MAX is a space intelligence simulation for the classic Divide & Conquer minimum/maximum algorithm. Mother Station splits incoming cargo across daughter stations recursively until each holds a single packet. Stations report their local minimum and maximum back up the chain, merging two comparisons at a time, until Headquarters compiles the final transmission.

The Naive Scan protocol instead sends a single patrol drone in a straight line across the array, checking every packet twice — once against the current minimum, once against the current maximum.

Because the Divide & Conquer protocol resolves elements in pairs, it saves one comparison per pair versus the naive approach, requiring only ≈3n/2 − 2 comparisons instead of 2n − 2.
